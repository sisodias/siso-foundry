# Agency intelligence backlog

This is the continuing evidence and promotion queue for Foundry's SISO Agency OS mission.

## P0 — make the intelligence operable

0. **Value-matrix expansion** — apply the `repository × use case × adoption route` contract to the existing source-read dossiers and 707 proof selections, record authority-group overlap, and stop only when every high-signal Agency capability has a scored row or an explicit rejection.

1. **Source Oracle API** — expose read-only, tenant-neutral queries over repository identity, categories, adoption evidence, capability dependencies, contract cards, and verification receipts. Do not expose raw operational database access.
2. **Snapshot receipts** — version external corpus snapshots with schema version, content hash, rights review, privacy review, generator revision, and at least two durable-copy receipts.
3. **Adoption truth completion** — resolve the 879 unresolved adoption records and attach evidence dates, source revisions, and confidence to decisions.
4. **Dependency persistence** — make capability and composition edges first-class evidence so agents can distinguish hard requirements, soft complements, conflicts, and replacement candidates.

## P1 — prove the Agency OS path

5. **Module dossiers** — directly review each Agency candidate cluster for source revision, license, maintenance, security, tenancy, data ownership, API coverage, deployment burden, and integration mode.
6. **Capability adapter proof** — integrate one representative module through SISO identity, capability, approval, audit, metering, and event contracts; compare this with direct embedding.
7. **Control-plane ownership** — register or identify the owning SISO Agency Work before promoting the six recurring business-glue gaps out of Foundry.
8. **Outcome loop** — record cost saved, time saved, quality, risk, and agent autonomy achieved by every deployed capability, then feed the result back into adoption evidence.

## P2 — scale across industries

9. **Industry maps** — turn recurring business jobs, regulations, data objects, and source modules into Library-visible industry capability maps.
10. **Portfolio sweeps** — evaluate high-signal repositories by capability gap and business outcome, not stars alone.
11. **Owner promotion** — move verified patterns and packages to their owning Works; Foundry keeps evidence and lineage, not duplicate product source.
12. **Retirement loop** — explicitly retire stale candidates and superseded verdicts so agents do not keep re-deriving dead paths.

## Promotion rule

Discovery is not release. A candidate advances only through direct source review, rights review, behavior or integration proof, owner assignment, an immutable release, Library indexing, and—when it belongs in Agency OS—an explicit capability adapter with policy and audit evidence.
